The planned workflow

A copy in Drive, before local cleanup.

  1. 01

    Save a finished strip

    The camera keeps the finished strip and its individual photographs. Only the finished strip is intended for cloud backup.

  2. 02

    Upload when connected

    With the owner’s permission, the service will upload to a private folder in that owner’s Drive and verify that the saved copy matches.

  3. 03

    Clear local copies after 30 days

    A strip and its associated individual photos will become eligible for local removal only after the retention period and a verified Drive copy. Unverified or incomplete sessions stay protected.

Your account.
Your archive.

Google’s permission screen lets the owner choose whether to connect. The app requests access to files it creates or that are explicitly made available to it, rather than the entire Drive.

The planned service will not delete cloud copies. They stay in Drive until the owner removes them. If Drive is full or a backup fails, unbacked local files remain on the camera.

A finished strip is not a full-resolution backup of its individual photographs. Once the approved local cleanup removes those originals, the strip is the retained image.

About this project

A personal-use companion to the owner’s photobooth, not a public storage service. This informational website does not connect accounts or receive photo uploads.
