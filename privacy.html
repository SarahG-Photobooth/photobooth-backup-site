Data & privacy

Privacy policy

A personal archive, with clear limits on what is saved and shared.

Policy updated 1 November 2026 · Planned features are identified below

What is available today

The camera stores images locally. A laptop authorization helper has been prepared; successful Google authorization has not yet been confirmed. Automatic cloud uploads and local retention cleanup are not implemented. Future practices below are labelled as planned.

1. Who this policy covers

Photobooth Backup is a personal-use backup companion for a Raspberry Pi photobooth. It is intended for the camera owner’s Google account, not general public registration. People photographed use the camera; they do not need to sign into Google.

Operated by the camera owner. For questions about this app or your data, contact sarah.connor919@gmail.com.

2. Data used during connection setup

The laptop helper requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows access to app-created files and files explicitly made available to the app, rather than blanket access to every file in the account. The permission is broader than upload-only; the planned application will not use it to delete cloud copies.

After consent, the helper checks the account email and Google account identifier to confirm the intended owner. It also requests storage-quota information, when available, to understand capacity. It stores authorization and refresh tokens so a future installed service can connect without repeatedly asking the owner to sign in.

The helper does not request or save the Google account password. Passwords and verification codes are entered only on Google’s sign-in pages.

3. Photo data and its purpose

The planned backup service will upload finished photo-strip JPEGs, not their individual source photographs, to a private folder in the owner’s Drive. It will keep file identifiers, sizes, integrity checksums, timestamps and verification records to confirm copies and safely resume interrupted work. It may read back an uploaded strip to verify it.

Individual photographs remain local and may be linked to their finished strip for the approved retention rule. Incomplete or cancelled sessions without a verified finished strip will not be automatically removed under that rule.

4. Where information is stored

Today, Google client configuration and any successfully accepted authorization record are intended to stay in a private laptop folder, outside the source-code repository. The helper restricts Windows file access to the owner, system and administrators. It does not itself encrypt these files at rest.

A future Pi installation will need separately approved protected credential storage. Finished cloud strips will be stored in the owner’s Google Drive, subject to Google’s security and storage practices. The camera also retains local images and operational records. Owner-created laptop backups are separate copies and are not automatically removed by camera cleanup.

5. Retention and deletion — planned

The intended local retention period is at least 30 days from the session’s creation. Age alone will not authorize deletion: the service must also verify an unchanged, accessible cloud copy of the finished strip before removing its local copy and associated individual photographs.

The strip preserves the layout and images shown in it, not full-resolution copies of the individual originals. The owner has chosen not to upload those individual photographs. Once their eligible local copies are removed, they cannot be restored from the strip as the original files.

Offline operation, full Drive storage, revoked access, missing cloud copies or failed verification will postpone deletion. Unrelated files, designs, preferences and required safety records are outside routine media cleanup.

The app will not automatically delete or trash files in Drive. The owner decides when to remove them. If the owner moves a cloud strip to an external drive and deletes the Drive copy before local cleanup, that external copy is not automatically verified by this service; the remaining local copy stays protected pending owner review.

These automatic retention features are not active today. The previous, separately approved laptop-backed cleanup was a one-time maintenance operation.

6. Sharing and restricted uses

The service is intended to send strips to Google Drive for the owner’s archive, without creating public links or granting other accounts access. The owner can independently change sharing in Drive and is responsible for those choices. Existing sharing settings should be checked before the backup folder is used.

Photobooth Backup will not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or training general-purpose AI models. It will not send photographs or authorization tokens to this informational website. The planned application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

A trusted maintainer may assist the owner with setup. Credentials and private images should not be placed in chat, public support reports or the source-code repository.

7. Disconnecting and requesting help

The owner can review or remove the app’s access using Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access; it does not delete existing strips from Drive, the camera or laptop backups. The owner can manage Drive copies directly. Removing local credentials or camera records requires a separately reviewed maintenance action.

If an account check or backup fails, keep the original files and seek help rather than clearing credentials or verification records to force a retry. For help, email sarah.connor919@gmail.com.

8. This website

This website contains static information only. It has no upload form, Google sign-in, analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by its own code, or remote fonts. Visiting it does not grant access to Drive.

The website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser details, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This website does not receive your photo strips or Google authorization tokens.

9. Changes to this policy

The policy must be reviewed before new backup features are activated. Any material change to Google-data access or use will be explained to the owner, and additional consent requested where needed, before that change is used. The published policy will carry its effective date.
